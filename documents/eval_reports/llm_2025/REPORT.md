# 2025 season, LLM mode: what the shipped system recommends

Generated by `scripts/report_llm_2025.py`. Sample and protocol: `documents/audits/MEASUREMENT_2025_METHODOLOGY.md`. Session record: `documents/audits/MEASUREMENT_SESSION_2025_LOG.md`.

**Population, stated once and applying to every rate below:** rail-eligible green-flag pit stops of the 2025 season, drawn by a seeded uniform draw (`seed 20250806`, at most 3 windows per race-lap) from a nine-race design covering all four circuit clusters. It is **not** a season-wide figure and it is not comparable to the published `decision_modes.md` numbers, whose sample is different.

**Races each arm ACTUALLY covers**, which is not the design and must not be quoted as it: LLM arm 7 (Barcelona, Budapest, Lusail, Mexico_City, Monaco, Montréal, Monza); deterministic arm 9 (Barcelona, Budapest, Lusail, Mexico_City, Monaco, Montréal, Monza, Silverstone, Suzuka).
